# Community code of conduct

## Our standards

Project participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, discuss ideas rather than personal attributes, give actionable technical feedback, and respect privacy and attribution.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and bad-faith use of project communication channels.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact repository owner `@narain1`. If a report must remain private, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel and clearly label the report as a conduct matter. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
